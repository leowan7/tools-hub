Questions

What a Workspace covers.

What does a Workspace include?

Activate one target PDB and run as many design jobs as you want for 30 days, against a $100 (Standard) or $500 (XL) Modal compute budget. All pipelines — RFdiffusion, BindCraft, RFantibody, BoltzGen, MPNN, AF2, ColabFold — work on that target inside the Workspace. Every result page shows the actual scores (ipTM, pLDDT, i_pAE, developability) — no stubs.

What if I need more than 2,000 designs?

For most BindCraft / RFdiffusion campaigns, 500–2,000 in-silico designs is more than enough — modern pipelines find nanomolar binders from just 10–100 experimentally screened candidates (Pacesa et al., Nature 2025). For industrial-scale sweeps with 10k+ designs, activate Workspace XL instead — $500 Modal budget, priority GPU queue.

What if my target is hard?

Your first Workspace is covered by a 7-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. Use Epitope Scout (free) to score feasibility before you commit, and click the refund button on the workspace dashboard within 7 days if the output isn't what you needed.

Can I run multiple targets in one Workspace?

No — a Workspace is scoped to a single target so the compute budget stays predictable. If you have several targets, activate one Workspace per target. The Workspace dashboard tracks them side-by-side.

What happens after 30 days?

The Workspace expires and new submissions stop. All your finished results and PDB downloads remain readable in your account — you don't lose data. To keep iterating, activate a new Workspace on the same target.

Can I run on my own GPUs?

Not today. Every tool runs on Modal infrastructure we manage and validate — that's the tradeoff for published audit logs and predictable runtimes. For self-hosted workflows the underlying models (RFdiffusion, BindCraft, RFantibody, BoltzGen, MPNN) are all open source.
