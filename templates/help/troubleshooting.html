Troubleshooting

Common problems and how to fix them. If a fix does not work, email support and we will look at the job with you.

Problem Fix
PDB validation failed or no atoms found The uploaded file is not a parseable structure or has no atom records. Re-export a clean PDB or mmCIF from your viewer, make sure it contains ATOM lines, and upload the structure rather than a header-only or truncated file.
Still stuck? Contact support
Chain not found The chain ID you entered is not in the file. Open the structure and check the exact chain labels, which are case sensitive, then enter one that exists. If your file has a single unnamed chain, rename it before upload.
Still stuck? Contact support
Job failed or pipeline error Run a smoke run of the same tool first to confirm your inputs are accepted, then reduce the scale and resubmit. If a smoke run also fails, the inputs are the likely cause. Send us the job ID and we will check the logs.
Still stuck? Contact support
Job timed out The run exceeded its time budget, usually from too large a design count or an oversized target. Lower the number of designs or trim the input to the relevant chains, then resubmit at a smaller scale.
Still stuck? Contact support
3D viewer blank Reload the job page and let the viewer finish loading before you interact with it. If it stays blank, try a different browser or disable extensions that block WebGL, since the viewer needs hardware acceleration.
Still stuck? Contact support
Wallet balance too low The estimated cost of the job is above your available balance. Top up your wallet, the minimum top-up is $20, then resubmit. You can also pick a smaller preset to lower the estimate.
Top up your wallet · Still stuck? Contact support
