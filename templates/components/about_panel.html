What good looks like

Each tool reports a subset of these depending on whether it does design, sequence recovery, or structure prediction.

ipTM
{# Same correction as templates/help/tool_guide.html, and this is the copy's THIRD home — an independent review found two of the three. "The binder to target interface" is the metric's intent, not what every tool stores: on a multi-chain target rfdiffusion, pxdesign and bindcraft report a number computed over the whole complex, and so did boltzgen before the August 2026 container update. Predicted confidence at an interface. Higher is better; aim above roughly 0.7 on a tractable target. On a multi-chain target it may cover the target's own chain–chain interface as well as the binder's.
pLDDT
Per-residue confidence in the predicted fold. Higher means the model is more sure of that part of the structure.
i_pAE and pAE
Predicted alignment error, at the interface (i_pAE) or across the whole structure (pAE). Lower is better.
ProteinMPNN recovery
Fraction of native residues recovered when ProteinMPNN redesigns a known sequence on its native backbone. Higher is better; well calibrated above roughly 0.4 on diverse folds.
