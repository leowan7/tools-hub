How long each new binder may be. Every design draws its own length from this window, so the structures that come back are not all the same size: a wide window buys variety, a narrow one consistency. 60 to 120 is the default and a reasonable first try. Narrow it towards the short end for a compact single-domain binder on a flat patch, and widen it for a broad face or a groove a short binder cannot reach across. Longer binders are also harder to express and purify at the bench. 20 to 300 allowed.
