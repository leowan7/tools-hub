How it works Full pricing →

We send a quote and an invoice with our bank details. When the transfer arrives we add the credit to your wallet, and it works exactly like a card top up from there: the balance is your spend cap, jobs debit only the compute they deliver, and the money does not expire.

A bank transfer takes a few days to clear, so this is the slower of the two routes. If you want to start a run today, a card top up credits your wallet in seconds.
