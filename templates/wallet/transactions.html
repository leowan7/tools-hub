{# Legend explaining the kinds for first time users. #}
What the kinds mean
signup credit
One time $5 trial credit on first account creation.
topup
Manual top up via Stripe Checkout. Net of Stripe fees and tax.
auto reload
Off session charge to your saved card when balance dropped below your threshold.
hold
Pre authorization at the estimated cost of a job. Frees up if the job is cheaper than expected.
hold release
The surplus from a hold being returned to your wallet after settlement.
charge
Final debit for compute used by a job. References the hold above.
absorbed variance
Ranomics absorbed cost when actual exceeded the hold and your wallet could not cover it. Tracked for transparency, never debited.
promo
Manually granted credit (campaign, support gesture, partner offer).
adjustment
Manual correction by Ranomics ops. Used rarely, always accompanied by a note.
refund
Stripe refund issued. Reduces wallet balance by the refunded amount.
